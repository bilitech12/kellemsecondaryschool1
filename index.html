ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A place to learn, grow and serve the community.

Kellem Secondary School is a high school located in Dambi Dollo. The school supports students through Natural Science and Social Science departments and encourages a culture of learning, discipline, creativity and continuous improvement.

“Read every day for at least 10 minutes. Take small steps and make big changes.”
